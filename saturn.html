🪐 Saturn

The jewel of our solar system, renowned for its spectacular rings.

Type

Gas Giant

Avg Temp

-140°C (-220°F)

Moons

146 Confirmed

Orbit Period

29.5 Earth Years

Overview

Saturn is the sixth planet from the Sun and the second-largest in our solar system. Made mostly of hydrogen and helium, it is encircled by thousands of beautiful ringlets composed of chunked ice and rock.
