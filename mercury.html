🪨 Mercury

The swift, crater-pocked world closest to the Sun.

Type

Terrestrial (Rocky) Planet

Day Temp

Up to 430°C (800°F)

Night Temp

Drops to -180°C (-290°F)

Orbit Period

88 Earth Days

Overview

Mercury is the smallest planet in our solar system and the closest to the Sun. Despite its proximity to the Sun, its lack of a substantial atmosphere means it cannot trap heat, resulting in extreme temperature swings between day and night.
