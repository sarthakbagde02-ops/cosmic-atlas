Overview

Jupiter is the fifth planet from the Sun and by far the largest planet in our solar system—more than twice as massive as all other planets combined. Made mostly of hydrogen and helium, it is famous for its swirling atmospheric stripes and the Great Red Spot, a giant storm that has raged for centuries.
